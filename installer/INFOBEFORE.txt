Welcome to Media Recommendation Engine

This installer sets up a local recommendation app on your PC.

What gets installed
- Application files under Program Files
- Background services (database, cache, API, workers)
- Your data and settings under ProgramData (kept across upgrades)

You do not need Docker, WSL, or a separate Python install.

After setup, open the Start Menu shortcut to use the app in your browser
(http://127.0.0.1:8000/).
